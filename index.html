Full History Export

Every day in one file: weight, body fat, meals, workouts (every set), activity, and notes from you and your trainer, plus your goals, meal plan and training rotation. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or another AI and ask for advice.

A long history can take 10–20 seconds to prepare the first time; after that it's quick.

Import past weights

Paste one line per day: date, weight, body fat. You can copy straight from Excel, Google Sheets or a scale app. 2025-03-14 or 14/03/2025 both work. Body fat is optional.

APP

How to use

Progress: your weight and body fat trends, photos and every entry.

Today: pick a day on the calendar, then log Weight, Meals (tick what you ate from your plan) or Workout (body parts, then sets). Notes with your trainer are at the bottom.

Planner: your meal plan, workout rotation and goals.

Your photo (top right): edit your profile, dark mode, share or export, change PIN, sign out.

Changes save automatically.

Add to your home screen

  1. Open this app in Safari (in LINE: ⋯ → Open in Safari).
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. It gets this icon and opens full-screen, like a normal app.
